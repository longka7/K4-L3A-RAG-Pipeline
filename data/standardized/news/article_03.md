# VinUniversity's Ph.D. in Computer Science Scholarship - now open for 2025-2026 admissions

March 17, 2025

The admission cycle for VinUniversity's Ph.D. in Computer Science program for the 2025-2026 academic year officially kicks off on March 1st, 2025! With generous 100% scholarships and an ideal research environment, VinUniversity is seeking talented Ph.D. candidates.

## Scholarships

- Full sponsorship of PhD program tuition and monthly allowance up to 20,000,000 VND/month.
- Study duration: 3-4 years, full-time.

## Exclusive Benefits for Doctoral Students

- 100% of students have the opportunity to apply for Vingroup's 1,100 scholarship program to participate in research for 1-2 years at a VinUniversity partner university (covers all fees, tuition, living expenses, health insurance, round-trip airfare).
- Students are directly guided by leading professors, in the top 2% of most cited professors across all scientific fields, plus co-supervision by world-famous professors from partner universities.
- Access to state-of-the-art facilities: super labs, 24/7 project labs, rapid processing rooms, and library access to IEEE and ACM databases.

## Admission conditions

- Graduated with a Master's degree or a Bachelor's degree with honors or higher in Computer Science or related fields.
- Research experience via a master's thesis, published scientific articles, or 2+ years working as a lecturer/researcher.
- A draft research outline and expected study/research plan.

## English requirements

- IELTS 6.5 or higher (or equivalent) within 2 years before enrollment/graduation; or IELTS 5.5 at admission with a valid 6.5+ certificate supplied within 12 months.

## Admission application documents

- Statement of Purpose (max 2 pages), CV, official transcript, IELTS certificate, 2 letters of recommendation, links/scans of published articles (if any).

## Admission process and timeline

- Enrollment target: 10 graduate students for 2025-2026.
- Application deadline Round 1: until April 30th, 2025. Round 2: TBA.
- Interview & results: 1 month after submitting application.
