# Scholarships awarded to three Vietnamese research students to study at the University of Sydney

August 29, 2023

The University of Sydney and the Vingroup Scholarship Program partner on new scholarships. PhD scholarships for Vietnamese students will support innovative research across health and robotics.

To advance Vietnam's science and technology industry, a group of Vietnamese students have received a prestigious scholarship to undertake their PhD studies at the University of Sydney. Their goals include advancing vehicle localisation, improving methods for diagnosing frontotemporal dementia (FTD), and finding new treatments for skin cancer.

The PhD candidates will undertake projects across the faculties of Engineering, Science and Medicine and Health. The USYD Vingroup scholarship will cover their tuition fees, travel expenses and a living stipend for the duration of their candidature. It is co-funded by Vingroup and the University of Sydney.

The recipients for 2023 include Tran Hoang Khoi Nguyen (Faculty of Engineering), Do Tran Minh Hiep (Faculty of Science) and Luong Nguyen Dac Thuy (Faculty of Medicine and Health).

Nguyen Tran hopes to make self-driving vehicles more reliable by facilitating robust vehicle localisation and mapping for different environments by fusing diverse sensing modalities.

Hiep Do is researching translational clinical outcomes for FTD by combining machine learning analysis and multimodal neuroimaging, aiming to improve the clinical outcome of FTD patients.

Thuy Luong will focus his PhD on the genetic mechanism of squamous cell carcinoma, a common form of skin cancer, seeking novel therapies to improve treatment efficacy and survival prognosis.

All three scholars expressed a shared goal of returning to Vietnam after their studies to contribute to the country's development in robotics, mental/brain health science, and cancer treatment respectively.
