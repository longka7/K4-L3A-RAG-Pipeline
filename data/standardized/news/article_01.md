# 10 VinUni students awarded the 2026 Canada-ASEAN SEED Scholarship

August 23, 2026

Global competitiveness is not built in the classroom, it is reflected in students' ability to thrive for, and succeed in, opportunities on the global academic stage.

This year, 10 VinUni students have been awarded the Canada-ASEAN Scholarships and Educational Exchanges for Development (SEED) 2026, funded by the Government of Canada to provide students from ASEAN countries with opportunities to study or conduct research at Canadian post-secondary institutions.

In Fall 2026, our SEED Scholars will embark on semester exchanges at leading Canadian universities:

- University of Toronto: 3 students
- Queen's University: 2 students
- Polytechnique Montreal: 4 students
- York University: 1 student

With this year's cohort, 21 VinUni students have now been awarded the SEED Scholarship, reflecting the growing ability of VinUni students to compete for international opportunities and engage with academic communities beyond Vietnam.

From VinUni to universities around the world, our students are expanding their academic horizons, building global networks, and gaining the experience and confidence to learn, work, and contribute across borders.

Congratulations to our 2026 SEED Scholars, and we wish them an inspiring journey in Canada!
