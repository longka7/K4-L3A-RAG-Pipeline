CONDITIONS TO RETAIN SCHOLARSHIP

The student will play an active role in retaining the Scholarship. At the end of each
academic year, the student needs to submit a self-evaluation report on: (1) Academic
performance; (2) Compliance of Student Regulations; (3) Values/Attributes according
to our institutional values EXCEL; (4) Specific proposal on retaining or upgrading your
Scholarship.

The  Scholarship  will  be  automatically  renewed  if  the  student  ensures  that  his/her
academic  performance  achieves  at  least  the  Very  Good  level  (for  100%  and  Full
Scholarship),  Good  level  (for  90%  Scholarship)  and/or  the  student  expresses
outstanding  or  unique  abilities  and  that  the  student  does  not  face  severe
disciplinary action due to violation of Student Regulation.

In  case  that  the  student  does  not  meet  the  stated  requirement  to  retain  his/her
Scholarship, VinUniversity will evaluate the student’s performance according to our
institutional values (EXCEL) to decide on Scholarship status.
