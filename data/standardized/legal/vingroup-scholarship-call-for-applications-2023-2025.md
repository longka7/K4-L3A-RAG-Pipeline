SCHOLARSHIP ANNOUNCEMENT:

The Vingroup Science & Technology Scholarship Program
For Overseas Study for Master’s and Doctoral Degrees
For Academic Years 2023-2024 and 2024-2025

I.  ABOUT

***

The Vingroup Science and Technology Scholarship Program for Overseas Study for Master’s and
Doctoral Degrees (the ‘Scholarship Program’) was established by Vingroup with a mission to
find talents and provide them with rewarding opportunities so that they can to lead and advance
the development of science and technology in Vietnam in the future. The official information about
the  Scholarship  Program  is  available  at  https://scholarships.vinuni.edu.vn/.  The  Scholarship
Program is managed and administered by VinUniversity (https://vinuni.edu.vn/).

For 11 years starting from 2019, Vingroup will provide up to 100 full scholarships per year for
Master’s  and  Ph.D.  degree  programs  for  Vietnamese  talents  to  study  key  areas  at  excellent
universities in leading countries in terms of science and technology including Australia, Canada,
France,  German,  Israel,  Italia,  Japan,  Russia,  Singapore,  South  Korea,  Switzerland,  the  United
Kingdom, and the United States.

Applicants for the Scholarship Program can apply following one of these two tracks:

▪  Track 1: For applicants who have already obtained letters of admission to study a Master’s
or a Ph.D. program in AY 2023/24 in a supported field or a research area as per Section II
of this announcement and at a university among the List of Targeted Universities in Section
III below.

▪  Track 2: For applicants who have not been accepted to study a Master’s or a Ph.D. program
at any of the targeted universities and choose to apply for the scholarship before applying
for admission to study in AY 2024/25.

In 2023, the Scholarship Program will award up to 10 full scholarships for the Master’s program
in Cryptology and Information Security (CRYPTIS) at the University of Limoges, France. Further
information  can  be  found  at  https://scholarships.vinuni.edu.vn/master-cryptis-scholarship-
program/

science  and  technology  in  Vietnam.  There  is  also  a  great  demand  for  high-quality  human

III.

LIST OF TARGETED UNIVERSITIES

The  Scholarship  Program  identified  a  list  of  targeted  universities  that  have  excellent  graduate
programs  in  the  world  in  the  supported  fields  of  study  in  developed  countries  in  science  and
technology.  Applicants  need  to  pursue  one  of  the  supported  fields  of  study  at  the  targeted
universities  of  the  Scholarship  Program.  The  list  of  targeted  universities  of  the  Scholarship
Program is available at https://scholarships.vinuni.edu.vn/list-of-targeted-universities/

IV.

SUPPORT FROM THE SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM

The  Vingroup  Scholarship  Program  provides  fully  funded  scholarships  that  cover  all  costs  of
attendance for the standard duration of a graduate degree program in accordance with the policy
and regulations of each university, which is usually 2 years for a Master’s degree and 4-5 years for
a Ph.D. degree. The grant amount of each scholarship is different depending on the tuition and fees
policies of each program/university and the cost of living in each country/region. To specify, each
scholarship will fully cover the following expenses:

1.  Tuition and fees: All tuition and fees for the standard duration of study determined by the
Host  university  and  in  line  with  the  cost-sharing  agreement  between  the  Scholarship
Program and the Host university, if available.

2.  Monthly stipend/Monthly salary:  To support the monthly living expenses. The actual
amount  of  monthly  living  stipend/monthly  salary  depends  on  the  Host  university’s
regulations for the standard minimum rate of living expenses for international students.

3.  Travelling Allowance: Including allowance for a round-trip air ticket from Vietnam to the

Host university abroad at the beginning and at the end of the degree program.

4.  Visa: One-time visa application fee before commencing the approved study program. This
does not include transportation, lodging, or other expenses related to the process of getting
one’s visa.

5.  Student health insurance: Required international student health insurance for the standard

duration of the degree program according to the regulations of the Host university.

6.  One-time allowances: Including settling-in allowance and reintegration allowance after

graduation and return to Vietnam;

APPLICATION AND REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

The online scholarship application includes:

VII.  SELECTION PROCESS

The Selection process consists of three rounds:

▪  ROUND 1: Initial Application Screening

After  the  scholarship  application  deadline,  the  Scholarship  Program  will  conduct  an  initial
eligibility  screening  to  ensure  that  the  applications  meet  all  the  minimum,  pre-determined
requirements. After that, the Scholarship Selection Committee will review the applications and
shortlist the most suitable candidates for the interview round.

▪  ROUND 2: Interview

The  Scholarship  Selection  Committee  will  conduct  the  Interview  Round  which  will  be  in
English  and  last  up  to  45  minutes  for  each  interview  via  teleconference  or  face-to-face  if
circumstances permit.

▪  ROUND 3: Apply for Admission at Targeted Universities

After  passing  the  Interview  Round,  shortlisted  candidates  who  have  not  had  any  letter  of
admission from any Targeted University will apply to at least five (05) proposed universities
which  are  approved  by  the  Selection  Committee.  Candidates  who  obtain  at  least  one
unconditional  admission  offer  to  study  a  qualified  graduate  program  according  to  the
Scholarship Program’s policies will then be officially recognized as a Vingroup scholarship
recipient.

Note: Track 1 candidates who have already had admission offers from a supported field of study
at a Targeted University do not need to participate in Round 3 after the interview. All applicants
will be notified of final results.

IX.

SCHOLARSHIP CONDITIONS

Applicants must fully meet the application requirements of the Scholarship  Program as well as
having a letter of admission from a targeted university listed above to study a supported academic
field. Applicants must also meet the health requirements through a General Health Check Program
before commencing their studies and be issued a valid visa to study the program overseas. During
the study program, applicants must meet the minimum academic requirements determined by the
program as well as reporting their academic performance and research progress periodically to the
Scholarship Program

Scholarship  recipients  who  have  completed  their  final  academic  requirements  of  the  Master's
and/or doctoral degree are eligible to stay in the Host country to work in their field of study for up
to one year for Master’s graduates and up to two years for Ph.D. graduates. After that, scholarship
recipients must return to Vietnam to work full-time for a Vietnamese public university or research
institute  or  work  for  a  member  company/research  institute/training  facility  of  the  Vingroup
ecosystem for at least the duration of time equal to the duration of study that they had received the
scholarship to study the master’s or doctoral degree.

X.

REINTEGRATION SUPPORT

In  order  to  complete  an  overall  and  comprehensive  investment  process  in  the  science  and
technology  human  resources  in  Vietnam,  the  Program  will  support  scholarship  recipients
throughout  their  study  and  provide  them  with  regular  updates  on  employment  opportunities,
internships or research projects in Vietnam. This support scheme ensures that the awarded students
can be able to optimize their intellectual capability in an overseas university environment while
staying connected with the Vietnamese settings.
